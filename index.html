What is biological invasion?

Biological invasion is a process in which organisms are introduced beyond their natural ranges, establish populations and spread in new environments. Some introduced species become invasive and can affect native biodiversity, ecosystem processes and human well-being.

Understanding how invasions begin, how they spread and what impacts they create is an important step towards prevention, early detection and effective management.

Species spotlight

Wedelia trilobata

Common name: Singapore daisy

Wedelia trilobata is a fast-spreading groundcover native to Central America. It can form dense mats that suppress other vegetation and spread into gardens, wetlands and forest edges.
