Who we are

A platform to understand biological invasions

Bioinvasion is a knowledge and awareness platform initiated by TBARC to communicate the science and wider significance of biological invasions. It brings together accessible learning resources, species information, research highlights, field stories and community-oriented initiatives.

The platform aims to help students, researchers, educators, naturalists and the wider public understand how non-native species can establish, spread and affect ecosystems, and why prevention, early detection and informed management matter.

Biological invasions are an ecological issue that needs reliable knowledge, public awareness and collaborative responses.
Why this platform

Knowledge that leads to awareness

Biological invasion science spans ecology, biodiversity, policy and society. Bioinvasion aims to make these connections easier to explore.

Make science accessible

Present concepts, research findings and species information in a clear and approachable format without losing scientific context.

Build awareness

Improve understanding of invasive alien species, their pathways, potential impacts and the importance of responsible actions.

Encourage participation

Connect people with learning opportunities and citizen-science initiatives that support biodiversity documentation and awareness.

Our direction

Vision, mission and values

A shared direction for communicating invasion science and supporting informed biodiversity action.

Our vision

A society that recognises the ecological significance of biological invasions and supports the conservation of native biodiversity through informed decisions.

Our mission

To share reliable knowledge, communicate research, promote awareness and create opportunities for people to engage with invasive-species issues.

Our values

Scientific care, open learning, responsible communication, collaboration and respect for ecological complexity.
