A TBARC citizen-science initiative

Documenting invasive species, one observation at a time.

Invasive Watch Kerala brings together nature enthusiasts, students, researchers and citizen scientists to document invasive alien species found across Kerala.

Using iNaturalist, participants can upload observations with photographs and location information. These records help build a shared picture of where species are being observed and support awareness, learning and further research.

Important: An iNaturalist observation is a documented record, not by itself proof that a species is invasive. Species status and identifications should be interpreted using reliable scientific sources.

How to contribute

Anyone interested in nature and biodiversity can help improve documentation of invasive alien species in Kerala.

1. Observe and photograph

Photograph the organism clearly. Capture useful details such as leaves, flowers, fruits or other identifying features where possible.

2. Add your observation

Upload the observation to iNaturalist with the date and location. Choose the best identification you can and add it to the project where required.

3. Learn and collaborate

Engage with community identifications, improve your knowledge and help document invasive species records across Kerala.
